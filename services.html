Packages

Pricing

How engagements are scoped

Pricing is structured as a monthly retainer and tailored to the real complexity of the work, not a generic hourly estimate.

Factors we look at

  • How many providers or evaluators are involved
  • The volume and variety of referrals moving through the practice
  • The complexity of reporting, billing, and documentation coordination
  • How much system cleanup or workflow redesign is needed upfront
  • The level of ongoing operational ownership required

This lets us recommend the right level of support without over-scoping or forcing your practice into a package that does not match reality.
