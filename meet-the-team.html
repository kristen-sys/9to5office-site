Team Profiles

Meet the people who keep things moving

Founder & CEO

Kristen Prater

As the CEO and backbone of 9 to 5 Office, Kristen brings structure, strategy, and a deep respect for professionals' time to the often-chaotic world of practice operations. Known for her relentless follow-through, high standards, and color-coded-calendar-level organization, she leads with equal parts efficiency, heart, and Diet Coke.

Kristen believes systems should work for people, not the other way around, and approaches every initiative with optimism, clarity, and a strong bias toward action. If there is a better way to do something, she will absolutely find it.

Kristen and her husband, Joe, are the proud parents of their 10-year-old daughter, Ellie. Her superpower is getting a lot done with purpose and good humor. Think Leslie Knope energy minus the waffle obsession, plus operational excellence.

Chief of Operations & Creative Director

Sabrina Barnes

As COO and Creative Director, Sabrina lives at the intersection of organization and imagination. An ENFJ by nature, she thrives in problem-solving environments, bringing structure to complex systems while ensuring everything feels intuitive, thoughtful, and human.

When she is not streamlining workflows, Sabrina is a wife to her husband, Mikey, and a mom to her son, Luke. Neuropsychology holds a special place in her heart; navigating the diagnostic process for Luke gave her a deeper understanding of how life-changing clarity, the right supports, and thoughtful systems can be.

She is also a hands-on creative who finds joy in painting, woodworking, and building things from scratch, skills that translate seamlessly into her work: practical, creative solutions designed to actually be used.

Chief Financial Officer

Erica Harper

Erica has been the CFO at 9 to 5 Office for nearly six years, bringing a classic ENTJ "Commander" energy to the world of finance. While most people avoid paperwork, Erica thrives on it, specializing in liberating psychologists and neuropsychologists from the administrative chaos of invoice processing.

If there is a system to be optimized or a paper trail to be tamed, Erica is on the case. Outside the office, she applies those same observational skills to what she calls backyard psychology.

She is firmly convinced that every animal, from the local squirrels to her own Irish Setter, Aspen, and GSP, Jager, possesses a distinct personality and a likely dramatic backstory. Whether she is wrangling numbers or analyzing bird behavior, Erica is always the one with the plan and the data to back it up.
