About

Operational support built for practices that cannot afford messy systems.

9 to 5 Office supports forensic and evaluation-based practices by building structure around referrals, scheduling, reporting workflows, case visibility, and administrative operations.

Our Approach

We create operational calm in practices where complexity is part of the job.

Specialized practices often do not need more generic help. They need a partner who can bring structure to moving parts, create visibility across workflows, and support steady growth without increasing strain on clinical staff.

Embedded support

We integrate into the day-to-day work of the practice instead of operating as a disconnected vendor.

Operational ownership

We take responsibility for the systems, coordination, and follow-through that keep cases moving.

Structure over scramble

We replace inbox hunting, memory-based tracking, and reactive processes with organized workflows.

Professional discretion

We work in environments where timelines, documentation, communication, and confidentiality matter.

Our Story

A family-run business built around bringing order to chaos.

9 to 5 Office began with the goal of offering high-quality administrative support to growing businesses. Over time, the team expanded as more family members with strong administrative backgrounds joined the business, bringing experience from different industries and a shared instinct for organization, detail, and follow-through.

That family-run foundation still shapes the way we work: collaborative, reliable, and deeply invested in helping clients feel more supported behind the scenes.

What We Believe

Demand is not usually the problem. Operations are.

Many practices are successful enough to keep growing, but not yet structured enough to grow cleanly. That is where we come in. We help build the operational base that supports better consistency, clearer visibility, and less strain on the people doing the clinical work.

Growth should feel manageable

Scaling a practice should not mean every new referral creates more chaos behind the scenes.

Workflows should be visible

Your team should be able to see what is pending, active, delayed, and complete without digging.

Clinicians should stay clinical

Highly trained providers should not be spending evenings tracking tasks that operations can own.

Support should be consistent

Reliable operations come from systems, accountability, and ongoing oversight, not occasional help.
